Last updated: November 2, 2026

Euphory Ticket only stores what it needs to run your server's tickets, and never sells or shares that data for advertising.

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy explains what data the Discord bot Euphory Ticket stores, why, and for how long. It applies to everyone who uses the bot: server owners and staff, and players or users who open tickets.

  • Data controller: Gabriel Jesus Montalvo Vera (GJ Euphory), developer of Euphory Ticket.
  • Contact: GJ Euphory (@gjeuphory21) on Discord, or the support server.
  • Communities: each server (or pair of linked servers) that activates the bot forms a community. Its Owners decide how the bot is used there: which channels receive logs, whether AI is used and who can see each ticket.

2. What data the bot stores

The bot stores Discord IDs, display names and what people write in ticket forms. It does not store passwords, email addresses or payment details.

3. What it does not store, and where conversations go

Full ticket conversations are not stored by the bot: when a ticket closes they are sent to Discord and the bot removes them from its memory.

  • Transcripts: when a ticket closes, the bot reads the channel's messages, builds a transcript (an HTML file with messages and images) and posts it in the log channel chosen by the community. It also sends a copy by direct message to the staff member who closed it and, if the community enabled it, to the player. Then it deletes the ticket channel.
  • Files and images: they are downloaded only for a moment to re-upload them to the log. They are not kept on the hosting.
  • Messages outside tickets: the bot does not read or store messages from other channels.
  • Sensitive data: the bot does not ask for or store passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses or payment details.

Logs posted on Discord belong to the server where they were posted. Its Owners can delete them at any time.

4. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the ticket service inside each community:

  • Create the private channel for each ticket and give access to the right people.
  • Show the form and its answers to the staff.
  • Remember the player's platform and nickname so they don't have to type them on every ticket.
  • Enforce the community's rules: queue, ticket limits, cooldown between tickets, working hours and blacklist.
  • Generate the log, transcript, rating and staff statistics.
  • Send direct messages: queue turn, rating request, copy of the log and inactivity notices to Owners.

Special Discord permissions

  • Message Content: only inside ticket channels. Used to build the transcript and the AI summary when the ticket closes. Messages in other channels are ignored and never stored.
  • Server Members: to know who has each staff role, release the tickets of a staff member who leaves the server, and notify Owners when the community is inactive.

5. Artificial intelligence and other services

Each community decides whether to use AI. If it is enabled, when a ticket closes the bot sends the conversation text (display names and messages, no files) to one of these providers to write a summary and review the staff's handling: Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter, Google Gemini or Mistral.

The bot tries them one after another until one answers. Each provider handles the data under its own privacy policy. Important: the free plans of Google Gemini and Mistral may use the text they receive to improve their models. Each community's Owners can turn AI off completely or choose only the providers they prefer in /setup.

In addition:

  • Discord stores the ticket messages, channels and files, like any other Discord message.
  • The hosting service where the bot runs stores the data files described above.

No data is sold or rented to anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

6. How long data is kept

/setup change historyThe last 200 changes

When everything is deleted

  • If the Owners delete the community from /setup, its data is deleted immediately.
  • If the bot leaves a community's servers, its data is deleted 30 days later. This margin exists in case the bot was removed by mistake.
  • If a server never activates the bot, the bot leaves on its own after 7 days.

Messages the bot posted on Discord (logs, transcripts) are not deleted by this: they stay in those channels until someone on the server deletes them.

7. Who can see the data

  • Each community's staff sees the tickets and history of their own community, according to the permissions the Owners gave each rank.
  • The community's Owners see the settings and change history, and can download copies of the settings (without the AI keys).
  • The developer only accesses data when needed to provide support, fix a bug or comply with the law.

A community never sees another community's data.

Security

Data is stored on the server where the bot runs, with no public access. AI keys are never shown in full on Discord and are never included in backups. No system is 100% secure: if a problem affects your data, it will be announced on the support server.

Minors

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). Euphory Ticket follows the same rule and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe data from a minor was stored, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and contact

You can ask to:

  • See what data the bot stores about you.
  • Correct wrong data (for example, your nickname).
  • Delete your personal data from the history and player details.

To ask, write on the support server or contact the developer. If it concerns a specific community, you can also ask its Owners. We will reply within 30 days at most.

Some data may be kept if the community needs it for safety, for example a blacklist entry. Messages already posted on Discord depend on each server and on Discord.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the change will be announced on the support server. Continuing to use the bot after the announcement means you accept the new version.

This policy is also available in Spanish. If the versions differ, the Spanish version prevails.
