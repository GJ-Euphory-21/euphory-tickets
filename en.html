Ticket bot for Discord

Support that runs itself

Euphory Ticket opens a private channel for every request, with custom forms, staff ranks and complete logs. Built for Minecraft servers (Java & Bedrock) and for any community.

Add to Discord Support server

Everything your staff needs

Set everything up with buttons and menus from /setup, with help on every option. No commands to memorize.

Your own categories

Up to 15 categories: questions, reports, appeals, purchases or anything you invent, each with its own emoji and text.

Custom forms

Text, choices, files or a Discord member search. A general style, or a Minecraft style with nickname and platform.

Ranks and claims

Choose which rank sees each category. Every ticket has one owner, so two staff members never answer at once.

Opening rules

Waiting queue, ticket limits, working hours, cooldown between tickets and a blacklist.

Logs and transcripts

When a ticket closes, the bot posts a log with the full conversation, images and the user's rating.

AI summaries

Optional: a summary of every ticket and a review of how the staff handled it. Turn it off whenever you want.

Staff statistics

Tickets handled by each staff member, ratings and average duration, by week, month or year.

Secondary server

Link a second Discord server and receive the logs there, without cluttering your main server.

Set it all up with buttons

Type /setup and see what's missing, with a button to fix it. Ranks, channels, categories, forms and colors are changed with menus, and every option has its own Help. A 6-step wizard gets you ready in minutes.

Ready in 3 steps

The /setup wizard walks you through it: ranks, categories, log channel and panel.

  1. Add the botWith the “Add to Discord” button. You need the Manage Server permission.
  2. Type /setupPick your language and follow the wizard. You can change everything later.
  3. Post the panelYour members can start opening tickets.

Commands

/setupSet up the ticket system (Owners only)
/panelPost the ticket panel in the channel
/statsTickets, ratings and staff
/closeClose the current ticket
/add /removeAdd or remove someone from the ticket
/releaseRelease your claim on the ticket
/linkLink another server to your community
/infoBot information and support
